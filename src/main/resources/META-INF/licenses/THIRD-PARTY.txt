Third-party software in lws-authn
=================================

This JAR bundles the libraries below, most of them relocated under com.ebremer.lws.authn.shaded.
Relocation renames their packages; their code, copyright and licences are unchanged. The attribution
notices of the Apache-licensed libraries are merged into META-INF/NOTICE. target/bom.json, which the
build produces beside the JAR, lists the same libraries with their exact versions.
